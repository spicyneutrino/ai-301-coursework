# Rubric: is this plan ready to build?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| evidence-grounded-cause | Candidate diagnosis and quoted repro facts, compared with the repro-evidence steps, actual output, and issue | The proposed cause explains the reproduced behavior without contradicting its observations. The plan quotes or specifically cites the decisive repro observation; A mechanism inferred from the repro is sufficient when consistent with its controls and paired with a concrete source-tracing or debug-log step; it need not literally say "hypothesis". Contradicted causes fail. | required |
| bounded-scope | Candidate scope, exclusions, files and approach compared with the issue and repro | The change addresses one issue with bounded files or areas and an understandable stopping point. Related changes must be necessary for the fix or its verification; unrelated rewrites or extra features fail. Explicit exclusions are helpful but not mandatory when the boundary is already clear. | required |
| cause-directed-change | Approach compared with the diagnosis and repro mechanism | The proposed edit addresses the evidenced cause rather than hiding the symptom. A temporary workaround passes only when the issue or maintainer explicitly requests that workaround and the plan states its limits. | required |
| executable-approach | Candidate files or areas, concrete edits, sequence, and prerequisites | Another contributor can start implementing from the named location and intended operation without asking what to change. Exact line numbers and exhaustive pseudocode are unnecessary; Pinning exact functions during implementation is allowed when the subsystem, intended operation and concrete tracing method are already named. Vague promises to investigate or fix without a concrete operation fail. | required |
| observable-test | Candidate test plan compared with repro inputs, steps, expected and actual artifacts | The tests exercise the reported failure through the affected code or files and name an observable post-fix result that distinguishes success from the old behavior. Relevant shared-path regressions are checked when the package shows such a risk. A documentation-only issue can use file comparisons; unrelated tests or merely running the suite fail. | required |
| honest-uncertainty | Risks, unknowns, claimed results and deviations compared with available evidence | Claims stay within the evidence. Material unknowns are acknowledged with a way to resolve or contain them; A reasoned diagnosis may be stated directly when the plan supplies its observations and a concrete tracing step; unsupported guarantees of results or causes fail. No dedicated risks heading is required when the evidence shows no unresolved material risk. Recorded deviations explain what changed and why without pretending the old plan still describes the build. | required |
| thread-and-conventions | Candidate comment compared with thread highlights, repo facts and stated contribution policy | The comment communicates its own diagnosis, bounded change and verification clearly enough for the thread, respects applicable maintainer requests and stated contribution conventions, and includes tool and extent of AI assistance when the policy requires it. Silence in the policy creates no disclosure requirement. Another student's plan on the scoped classroom issue does not block this student's independent plan. | required |

## Verdict rule

Return accept only when every required check passes. Fail or unclear on any required check returns reject. A ? means unclear and counts as fail. Preferred checks, if added later, never change the verdict. Grade every check even after one fails.
